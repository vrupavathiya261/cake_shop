Since 2013

Every cake starts with a good story

Petal & Crumb began in a small home kitchen where our founder, Meera, baked cakes for friends and family every weekend. Word spread quickly — soon neighbours were placing orders, and what started as a hobby became a full-time passion.

Today we're a small team of bakers and decorators who still measure every cup of flour by hand and pipe every rose one petal at a time. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just honest, from-scratch baking.
